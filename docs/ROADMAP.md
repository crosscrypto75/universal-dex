# Roadmap

These sections describe evidence and plans, not dates or delivery promises.

## PROVEN

- Historical Solana DEVNET execution vertical: passkey-controlled account, zero-native-SOL vault start and sponsored execution.
- Deterministic validation, SPL operation and a real finalized Raydium swap, with independent 18/18 review and preserved public evidence.
- Reproducible read-only preparation/failure demo. Its nine fixture scenarios are SIMULATED, not live cross-chain settlement.

## IN PROGRESS

- Base Sepolia → Solana DEVNET CCTP vertical. Preparation and stage boundaries exist; complete settlement is NOT_PROVEN. Transport and Base total-native-debit guarantees remain unresolved gates. A separately finalized empty destination ATA is only a prerequisite.
- Runtime isolation qualification. PATH_B has partial lab provisioning and is FROZEN until after submission. It is not runtime-qualified; no real Nightly transport test is approved by this roadmap.

## NEXT

- Mobile client and user-facing UX, after the execution/security boundaries support it.
- Additional wallet and provider abstractions with separate chain authority and independent transport validation.
- Complete and independently verify the bounded testnet cross-chain ceremony, subject to separate human authorization for each live stage.

## LATER

- Additional chains and asset representations.
- Multi-chain liquidity routing based on total economic outcome.
- Cross-chain DeFi opportunity and capital routing.
- Risk-aware net-yield routing with explicit cost/risk assumptions and user limits.

All future routes must preserve self-custody goals, verifiability, permissionless access and the separation of provider, policy and signing authority. Production/mainnet readiness, recovery and independent exit require their own evidence.
