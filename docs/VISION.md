# Vision

Universal DEX aims to let users express economic intent: the asset or exposure they want, the capital they will commit, and the limits they accept. Chains, wallets, gas, bridges and liquidity venues should become infrastructure supporting that intent, with their risks and costs still visible and verifiable.

The long-term experience is mobile-first and provides access to markets across multiple chains without requiring users to manually orchestrate every funding and settlement step. A Universal Account would connect independent chain accounts without pretending that one credential automatically controls every network.

Beyond swaps, the same model could support DeFi capital routing: compare opportunities by net outcome after gas, bridge costs, liquidity, settlement delay and risk constraints. Future risk-aware net-yield routing would propose options rather than grant an agent authority over user funds. No yield or execution guarantee is implied.

Self-custody, verifiable execution and permissionless access are design goals. Providers remain replaceable and untrusted; local policy and explicit, bounded authorization remain distinct. Recovery, independent exit and upgrade assumptions must be proven before making stronger product-security claims.

This is the destination, not a description of shipped features. Today the proven execution boundary is one historical sponsored passkey Solana DEVNET swap; the cross-chain demo is preparation-only. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [current evidence](PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).
